01 — About

Biography

Shih-Hsun Chang (張士勳) is a full-time Associate Professor in the Department of Information Technology at Ling Tung University. His work combines a strong engineering foundation with modern computing, spanning intelligent systems, secure networks, and applied research.

He completed his doctoral and master's studies at National Cheng Kung University and holds a bachelor's degree from National Central University — a path that grounds his teaching in rigorous theory while keeping it connected to real-world application. In the classroom and the lab, he guides students toward building systems that are both intelligent and trustworthy.

His research interests center on artificial intelligence and machine learning, with a particular focus on network and information security, alongside interests rooted in aerospace engineering.
